WHY WE'RE HERE

Stories make room for us all.

StoryCast is an independent storytelling concept built around a simple idea: access should be part of the story from the beginning, not added at the end.

OUR MISSION

Make listening and watching feel welcoming.

We bring together personal audio and short-form video that help people see familiar experiences from a new angle. We value honest voices, thoughtful editing, and clear context so audiences can spend less time figuring out the interface and more time with the story.

StoryCast is a prototype microsite. The stories and artwork shown here are illustrative examples created for a student project, rather than a claim of published journalism.

◎

Access is part of the design

We aim to offer more than one way to follow every story.

  • Captions for spoken video and meaningful sounds
  • Readable transcripts for audio and video
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and visible focus
  • Clear headings, descriptive links, and meaningful alt text
  • Color contrast designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA
  • Responsive layouts with support for reduced motion

OUR PRINCIPLES

Designed with people in mind.

01

People are more than a headline. We give voices context and space.

02

03
